About

A workshop, not a showroom.

Sandbox is where I keep the things I build for myself — tools for the kitchen, for remembering, for staying on track — and the experiments that don’t belong anywhere else yet.

Some are polished. Some are half-finished. Each one is its own small, self-contained project with its own address, so feel free to wander, open anything, and come back later to see what’s changed.
